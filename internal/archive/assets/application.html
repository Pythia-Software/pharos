Move or copy history between libraries

History and its retained evidence are verified at the destination. The destination keeps its own saved preferences. A move empties delivered history from the source, including its offline search index. A backup is not required.

Both libraries pause while this operation runs. Large libraries need room for verified transfer staging.

Capture locally, transfer later

Choose a local pending library and an archive. Pharos captures the pending library’s approved sources while open, then moves its history to the archive when available. After delivery, the pending library is empty. The archive’s browsing selection and MCP assignment stay independent.

Configure source access in the pending library first. Its own source choices are used; source permissions are never copied from another library.

Where the application lives

Run Pharos from Applications for access whenever this Mac is on. Or keep Pharos.app on an SSD and launch that copy on each Mac; you do not need to install it on every Mac. To move the app, quit it and drag Pharos.app in Finder, then reopen it.

If the app is on an unplugged SSD, nothing captures or runs. No local history or history cache is kept by that portable app. Small update, window and integration registration settings may remain on this Mac.

Local capture while your archive is away requires an app and a pending library on this Mac. An SSD library alone cannot capture while unplugged.
