Ask in your own words

Ask Pharos lets you navigate the app with questions in plain language.

It uses your LLM’s native CLI integration. You’ll need Claude, Codex, or Antigravity installed and authenticated on this Mac.

Like any chat with your agent, each conversation consumes your account’s quota and will itself be indexed by Pharos.

Your Pharos page visits and earlier Ask Pharos chats are available to this private navigation agent. Page visits are never exposed through the general MCP service.

This is optional. No other use of Pharos involves AI. When Ask Pharos is disabled, Pharos will never access your agentic CLIs.
