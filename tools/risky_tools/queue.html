NIGHTJAR · experiment III · algorithmic selection

Cases worth investigating

Every card below is selected by versioned rules and filled from archived evidence. Each includes a bounded packet and an investigation prompt. No agent investigations have run.

Selection reasons first

Rule-selected cases · template-written descriptions

“Investigate” identifies stronger consequence or boundary signals. “Review” includes ambiguous operations. Neither is a finding of harm. Open a case to inspect its evidence and copy the prepared prompt. Cards rotate through rule groups to reduce repetition; this is not a risk ranking.

Inspect what the filter leaves out

Deferred means no current selection rule fired. It does not mean safe, authorized, or fully understood. Temporary paths have no automatic disposability guarantee; raw PIDs have no inferred ownership.

A reproducible sample for checking omissions

One sample comes from subject-pass calls without a selection trigger; some can still appear as related context. A separate sample comes from the earlier population-random cohort outside the subject pass. No audit labels or known case IDs drive this selection. These samples have not been reviewed in this experiment.

How a card is made

Structured actions → named triggers → related evidence → investigation packet.
Card titles, reasons, questions and uncertainty labels are fixed templates. Source facts and counts fill the cards. No model or hand-selected call IDs choose the cases.

Versioned selection rules

Boundaries of the experiment

  • This filters the prior 882 candidates plus five known audit misses. It is not a fresh scan of the week’s 80,759 calls.
  • Related calls share a conversation and compatible resource context within six hours of a seed. Association is not proof of causality.
  • Complex uncertain calls combine at least three consequential families into one case. This does not infer a broken heredoc; that is an investigation question.
  • Investigations are unrun. Authorization, effects, recovery, secret disclosure, cloud charges and remote audience remain unknown.
  • Source and result excerpts are redacted and bounded. Packets mark omitted and clipped evidence. Nearest user-role text is candidate context, not automatic permission.
  • Selection is deterministic for identical inputs and rules. Changes to parsers, rules or source evidence can change the queue.
